# Inception: Comprehensive Risk Register

## 1. Risk Matrix

| Risk ID | Risk Category | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Severity Score | Trigger Condition | Mitigation Strategy | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RSK-01** | **Performance** | `backdrop-filter` GPU cost causes frame drops, jank, or thermal throttling on mobile or low-end devices. | Medium | High | **High** | Scrolling with glass topbar/bottom pill on mobile devices. | Cap mobile blur radius to max 12px; limit total glass area to <15% on mobile; avoid animating blur radii or element dimensions; use `will-change: transform` sparingly. | Performance Lead |
| **RSK-02** | **Accessibility** | Text rendered on glass becomes unreadable or fails 4.5:1 contrast when scrolling over dynamic/busy backdrops. | Medium | High | **High** | Content scrolling directly beneath glass topbar/sidebar. | Enforce minimum 0.45 fill opacity; add 1px inner specular highlight; test against an automated 4-point worst-case backdrop matrix (white, black, indigo, gradient). | A11y Lead |
| **RSK-03** | **Compatibility** | Safari and Firefox render `backdrop-filter` with different color saturations, seams, or clipping glitches. | High | Medium | **High** | Cross-browser visual testing across macOS Safari, iOS Safari, Firefox, and Chrome. | Always declare `-webkit-backdrop-filter` before `backdrop-filter`; avoid combining `overflow: hidden` on transformed blurred parents in WebKit; provide robust fallback tests. | QA Lead |
| **RSK-04** | **Rendering Bug** | Nested `backdrop-filter` elements cause rendering artifacts or black rectangular patches in WebKit/Blink. | Medium | High | **High** | Modal sheet with glass surface placed over a page with glass topbar and sidebar. | Enforce architectural rule: Zero nested backdrop-filters. Modal scrims shall use standard semi-opaque dark fills (`rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`) without blurring the underlying window. | Architect |
| **RSK-05** | **Design / Aesthetic** | Overusing glass makes the UI look messy, chaotic, and generic rather than a clean, disciplined Apple interface. | Medium | Medium | **Medium** | Frosted cards applied to question text, options, or data tables. | Enforce **Rule 10 (Glass Discipline)**: Glass is restricted to chrome only (<25% viewport area). All questions, choices, explanations, and tables stay on solid, crisp white cards. | UX/UI Lead |
| **RSK-06** | **Functional Regression**| Visual restructuring unintentionally breaks 520-question scoring, option shuffling, timer, or local storage recovery. | Low | Critical | **High** | Refactoring `QuizScreen` or `LandingScreen` components. | Author automated characterization test suites (Vitest + React Testing Library) locking in existing state contracts *before* applying visual changes. | Dev Lead |
| **RSK-07** | **Bundle Bloat** | Introducing charting or icon libraries inflates the initial bundle beyond the strict 200 KB gzipped limit. | Low | High | **Medium** | Adding packages like Recharts, Chart.js, or Lucide icons. | Strictly adhere to ADR-0001: Build custom, lightweight SVG chart primitives (<5 KB total) and inline curated SVG icons. Zero new runtime dependencies. | Architect |
| **RSK-08** | **Accessibility Regression**| Focus rings, screen-reader announcements, or keyboard navigation break during glass UI component overhauls. | Medium | High | **High** | Replacing standard buttons with custom glass pills. | Implement high-contrast 2px `:focus-visible` offset rings; maintain semantic HTML elements (`<button>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`); run automated Axe-core scans on every build. | A11y Lead |
| **RSK-09** | **Scope Creep** | Attempting backend sync, new question authoring, or extra quiz modes delays delivery of the core visual redesign. | Low | Medium | **Low** | Requests to modify question banks or database persistence. | Strictly enforce Scope In / Scope Out boundaries defined in `intent.md`. No new business logic unless specced and approved. | Product Owner |
